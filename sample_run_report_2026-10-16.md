# Sample run report (2026-10-16, lightly edited)

A real report from one scheduled run, with company names removed from the skip list so it shows the *judgment*, not who was judged. The one role that came out as **Apply** is named, because it is the point of the example.

**Coverage** (numbers are only what was actually tallied in the run)
- Companies in master list: 438 (live career_url: 268 · empty: 8 · NOT_FOUND: 162)
- ATS companies re-fetched (Step 2): 69 rows / 64 unique slugs; 12 fetch errors, 10 boards returned 0 jobs
- Browser-only ATS (Hibob / Personio / Homerun): 13 / 13 loaded
- Custom career pages scanned (Step 4): ~165 / 186 (keyword/title scan; postings not counted)
- Not confirmed this run: login-walled pages (LinkedIn, Wellfound), one known-unreachable site, a handful of JS-only or empty pages. Listed explicitly rather than counted as covered.
- Candidate roles whose full JD was read: 9

**New role added to Notion**
- **[Apply] Minimal AI: Founder's Associate (Amsterdam, YC-backed).** Direct founder exposure, commercial and strategic ownership. The only strong bridge role of the week.

**Skipped after reading the full JD (examples of the rubric at work)**
- "Deployment Manager / Ops Manager": site-foreman and ops hybrid, not strategy.
- "Director, Expanded Access Operations": internal operations at a biotech.
- "Partnerships Manager": US-facing business development, quota-carrying.
- "Commercial Associate": really a BDR role under a gentler title.
- "Partnership Manager": sales in disguise.
- "Founder's Associate": turned out to be founding-GTM (sales) once the JD was read.
- "Deployment Strategist": measured on customer P&L, i.e. implementation.
- "Business Associate": already tracked.
- Not verified: "(Strategy) International Expansion": team blurb only, no posting or location (likely outside NL).

**Trends:** Boards remain GTM / ops / engineering heavy. One Chief of Staff posting disappeared between runs. The Minimal AI role remains the only strong VC bridge.

*Why this report matters:* a title filter would have surfaced most of the skipped roles ("Strategist", "Associate", "Partnerships"). Reading the JD is what separates them from the one that is actually worth applying to.
